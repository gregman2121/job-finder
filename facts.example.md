# Facts for cover letters

Copy this file to `my_facts.md` (git-ignored) and fill it in. The letter writer may only claim what is in
your facts file, and the checker flags any sentence that isn't backed by it. Be specific and honest:
the "Not yet" section is what keeps a letter from overselling you.

## Contact (used for the letter header, word for word)
Your Name
City, ST | (555) 555-5555 | you@example.com | linkedin.com/in/you

## Current role
- Title, employer, dates. Team, who you support, scale (sites, users, volume).

## Things he built or runs
- One line per project: what it does, the tools, and a real number if you have one.
- Say which ones you built and which you maintain. The writer follows your wording.

## Skills he can claim
- Languages, platforms, tools. Note limits, e.g. "SQL (basic)".

## Education and certifications
- Degrees and certifications with years. Mark expired ones.

## Personal
- Anything you'd use for one kind of job only, and when to use it.

## Not yet (never claim these)
- Tools and experience you don't have, so the letter never claims them.
